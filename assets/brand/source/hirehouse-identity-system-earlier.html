Transparent by default. Every copy and download ships with no background, in both SVG and PNG. The white and black panels above are only previews of the two grounds. Filled shapes that belong to a mark — the app icon tile, the pill, the badge field — are artwork and always export. Switch Ground to Solid only when you need a flattened PNG for somewhere that cannot handle alpha.

Light or dark. Pick by the ground you are placing on, not by taste. A dark asset is white ink, so on a transparent PNG it will look empty until it lands on something dark.

Which asset. Reach for 01 Primary horizontal by default. Drop to 05 Mark + wordmark when the space has height to spare, 08 App icon below about 120px, and 10 Favicon below 32px. Never shrink a wordmark past its minimum and hope.

Accent pairing. The four accents are not interchangeable across grounds. Blue #4C49F3 is the light-ground accent — 7.4:1 on white, only 2.9:1 on black. Lime #E9FEA3 and Yellow #FBFD78 are dark-ground accents — about 19:1 on black, effectively invisible on white. Lilac #CB9FD2 holds on black, marginal on white. Default to blue on light and lime on dark. The badge inverts, because its accent sits on the ink field rather than the page.

Text on an accent field follows the palette, not the mode: black on lime, yellow and lilac; white on blue. It never flips with light and dark.

Clear space. One cap height on all four sides, measured from the outermost letterform. The artboards carry about a quarter of that, so add the rest in layout.
Minimum sizes. Horizontal wordmark 90px / 22mm. Stacked 64px / 16mm. Mark 24px. Favicon 16px.

Never. Recolour "Hire" to an accent. Apply a gradient, shadow, outline or bevel. Stretch, condense or re-track the wordmark. Rebuild it in another typeface. Place the accent wordmark on a ground it fails contrast against. Add a tagline to anything but 06 Tagline lockup and 07 Social banner.
